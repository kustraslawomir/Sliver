# Google Play listing — Reelog

- **Title:** Reelog: 1s Video Diary
- **Short description:** One clip a day. One film a year.
- **Icon:** `icon-512.png` (from `brand/reelog/reelog-icon.svg`: red disc #FF3B30 on #0A0A0B)
- **Package:** `skustra.fragments` (kept from the earlier name; the OAuth client and existing backups depend on it)

## Full description (draft)
Record one short clip every day. Reelog keeps the best second of it and turns your days into a film.

- One clip a day, organised in projects ("My 2026", a trip, a person).
- Fill empty days from your gallery: the best camera video or photo of each day, checked in one tap.
- Make a film of the whole project with your own music and a filter.
- Backup and sync with your own Google Drive. No ads, no account needed, no tracking.
