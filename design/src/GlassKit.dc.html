Reelog v1.7 · glass & controls
Every control rebuilt: one type scale with nothing under 13 sp, one switch, one chip, one segmented control, one icon style. Glass only where something moves behind it — the camera, video, and the calendar scrolling under the dock. On a flat black screen glass is just grey, so plain surfaces stay plain. Orange is gone; the only colour is the record red.
1 · Type scale (Geist)
The old build used 11–12 sp captions and 14 sp list rows — too small, that's most of the "cheap" feel. New minimum is 13 sp. Rows are 17 sp like iOS / Pixel settings.

2 · Glass material
Real backdrop blur, not a grey fill. Built with Haze (dev.chrisbanes.haze, Compose Multiplatform): hazeSource on the content, hazeEffect on the glass. Android 12+ blurs for real; below API 31 it falls back to the solid fill in the table.
Glass · regular
Regular
Icon buttons, chips and controls over video. Blur 24 dp, tint white 8 %.
Glass · strong
Strong
Record button ring, dock, sheets. Blur 32 dp, tint white 12 %, deeper shadow.
Glass · light theme
Light theme
Tint white 62 %, white hairline, soft shadow. Text stays #141413.
BlurRegular 24 dp · strong 32 dp. Saturation 1.6–1.8 so colours behind stay alive instead of going muddy. TintDark: white 8 % / 12 %. Light: white 62 %. Noise 0.08 (Haze noiseFactor) — kills banding on the blur. Edge1 dp border, vertical gradient white 30 % → 6 % (lit from the top). That top highlight is what makes it read as glass, don't skip it. ShadowRegular 0/10/30 black 35 % · strong 0/16/40 black 45 %. None in light theme beyond 0/8/24 at 12 %. FallbackAPI < 31 or battery saver: solid #1C1C1F at 92 % (light: #FFFFFF at 92 %), same border. No blur, nothing breaks. WhereCamera (record ring, top icons, mode switch), film player & builder controls over video, day screen icons, the dock on the project screen, undo bar, bottom sheets. NOT on settings rows, lists, empty backgrounds. PerfStatic glass costs nothing until content behind it moves. No infinite animations on idle screens — the "Record today" glow that eats 60 % CPU on the emulator goes away completely.
3 · Record button · liquid glass
The liquid glow from v1.5 stays, but now it lives inside a glass lens: a strong-glass ring 88 dp around a 66 dp red core. The drifting blobs sit under the ring, so the ring catches coloured light from them and from the viewfinder. Camera only.
