SLIVER / STAGE 1 · BRAND / 02 — APP ICON DIRECTION A · 3 VARIANTS

The slit, at home-screen size.

01 — SLIT

Pure: one hot cut of light on true black. Most iconic in a big tile, but at 29px it shrinks to a hairline and starts to look like a loading bar.

02 — LIGHT LEAK RECOMMENDED

A door left ajar: the slit spills a wedge of warm light into the dark. Same motif, but more surface area, so it survives small sizes and owns a spot on a busy home screen.

03 — FRAME

Borrows C's frame around the slit, so the icon previews the trim window. Clear at any size, but the bone outline dominates and the light becomes secondary.

Plan: ship 02 as the default icon; offer 01 and 03 as alternate icons (iOS alternate icons / Android themed icon uses 01's single slit as the monochrome layer).
